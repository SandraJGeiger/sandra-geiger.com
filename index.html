How can we correct what people wrongly believe others think, and use it to speed up environmental action?

Research

I design and test theory-informed interventions, from messaging approaches and signaling interventions to conversational AI, to accelerate pro-environmental change across societies.

Much of my work starts from pluralistic ignorance: people underestimate how many others care about climate change and nature. I test whether communicating the real consensus corrects these misperceptions and raises support for climate and biodiversity policy, in large multi-country studies.

My PhD at the University of Vienna, completed in 2025, examined how to communicate pro-environmental consensus across countries. All preregistrations, data and analysis code are linked from my Open Science page.
